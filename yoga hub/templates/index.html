About Sangam Yoga Club

Welcome to Sangam Yoga Club, NIT Delhi's premier wellness community dedicated to promoting physical and mental well-being among students and faculty.

Our club brings together the ancient wisdom of yoga with modern academic life, creating a supportive environment where everyone can find their inner balance and peace.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced practitioner, our inclusive community welcomes all levels of experience. Join us in building a healthier, more mindful campus culture.
